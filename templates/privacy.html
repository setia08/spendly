Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when creating an account (name, email, password) and the expense data you log within the app (amounts, categories, dates, descriptions). We do not collect payment information.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the Spendly service — displaying your expense history, generating summaries, and personalising your experience. We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We use industry-standard measures to protect it from unauthorised access. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly uses Google Fonts for typography. No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services are integrated. We do not share your personal information with any external parties.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
